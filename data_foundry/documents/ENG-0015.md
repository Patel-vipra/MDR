# ENG-0015 Incident Postmortem: INC-073

## Impact

The incident designated as INC-073 affected the `auth-worker` system (System ID: SYS-004), which is a critical component managed under Project Juniper. The severity level was classified as Sev2, indicating a significant impact on system availability and performance. The disruption lasted for approximately 5.5 hours, during which authentication services experienced degraded functionality, potentially affecting user access and operational workflows. Given the high criticality of the `auth-worker` system, the incident posed a risk to ongoing project deliverables and operational stability.

## Timeline

- **2025-04-10:** The incident was reported by Michele Williams, a Marketing Specialist based in Singapore, at approximately 09:00 local time. The initial detection was linked to anomalies observed in authentication processes.
- **2025-04-10:** Crystal Robinson, Project Manager for Project Juniper and on-call engineer for SYS-004, was notified and began incident response activities promptly.
- **2025-04-10:** The root cause was identified as a configuration change applied to the `auth-worker` system, which led to the service disruption.
- **2025-04-10:** Resolution efforts commenced immediately, involving rollback procedures and configuration adjustments.
- **2025-04-10:** The system was fully restored and stabilized by approximately 14:30, marking the end of the incident response.
- **2025-04-10:** The incident was officially closed, with a total resolution time of 5.5 hours.

## Root Cause

The root cause of INC-073 was a configuration change implemented within the `auth-worker` system. This change was identified as the primary factor that led to the system's degraded performance and eventual service disruption. The change was executed without sufficient validation, which contributed to the failure to detect the adverse effects prior to deployment. The incident underscores the importance of rigorous change management and validation processes, especially for systems with high criticality like `auth-worker`.
