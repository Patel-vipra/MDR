# ENG-0013 Incident Postmortem: INC-066

## Impact

The incident involving the billing-pipeline system (System ID: SYS-010) was classified as Severity 2 (Sev2). The system, which is part of Project Echo, experienced a disruption that affected its operational stability. Given the medium criticality of billing-pipeline, the incident led to temporary delays in billing processes, impacting downstream financial reporting and customer invoicing. The resolution of the incident required 12.5 hours, during which operational capacity was compromised, necessitating immediate corrective actions to restore normal functionality.

## Timeline

- **January 13, 2025**: The incident was reported by Jeffrey Chavez, a Marketing Specialist, who identified issues with the billing-pipeline system. The incident was logged under ID INC-066.
- **Immediately following report**: On-call engineer Melissa Gates, Staff Engineer for the billing-pipeline system, was alerted to investigate the issue.
- **Investigation and containment**: The root cause was identified as a deployment error, which led to the disruption.
- **Resolution**: The deployment error was rectified, and the system was restored to normal operation.
- **Total resolution time**: 12.5 hours from initial report to full recovery.

## Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was determined to be a deployment error. This error originated during a recent update to the billing-pipeline system, which is built on a React/Node.js/DynamoDB tech stack. The deployment mistake was not caught during the deployment process, leading to the temporary failure of the system.
