# ENG-0005 Incident Postmortem: INC-089

## Impact

The incident involving the auth-worker system (SYS-004) on January 21, 2025, resulted in a Sev1 severity classification, indicating a critical disruption to the system's functionality. The auth-worker system, which is integral to our authentication processes, is designated as high criticality within our infrastructure. The disruption affected core authentication services, potentially impacting user access and system security protocols. The resolution of this incident took approximately 4.5 hours, during which operational stability was compromised, and user experience was adversely affected.

## Timeline

- **January 21, 2025**: The incident was reported by Jamie Arnold, Head of Data & Analytics, via internal channels. The initial detection indicated a significant failure within the auth-worker system.
- **Immediately following detection**: Crystal Robinson, Project Manager and on-call engineer for auth-worker, was mobilized to address the issue.
- **Incident Response**: The on-call engineer, Crystal Robinson, diagnosed the problem as stemming from a configuration change. The root cause was identified as a recent configuration modification within the auth-worker system.
- **Resolution**: The configuration change was reverted, restoring system functionality. The total resolution time was 4.5 hours.
- **Post-incident**: A review was conducted to understand the cause and prevent recurrence.

## Root Cause

The root cause of INC-089 was identified as a configuration change within the auth-worker system. This change was implemented without sufficient validation, leading to a failure in the authentication service. The change was likely introduced as part of ongoing updates under Project Juniper, which is managed by the owner project team. The incident underscores the importance of rigorous change management procedures, especially for systems of high criticality.
