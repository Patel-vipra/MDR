# ENG-0006 Incident Postmortem: INC-091

## Impact

The incident INC-091 affected the auth-gateway system, which is part of Project Fathom. Given its criticality level as medium, the system's unavailability or degraded performance potentially impacted user authentication processes across multiple services. The resolution of this incident took approximately 3.5 hours, during which time users may have experienced login disruptions or delays. The prompt resolution minimized the overall operational impact, but the incident underscores the importance of rigorous change management procedures for systems integral to our infrastructure.

## Timeline

- **Incident Reported**: The incident was reported by Gina Moore, Head of Sales, on 2025-05-27.
- **On-Call Engineer Activation**: Alyssa Long, Product Manager and on-call engineer for auth-gateway, was alerted and responded promptly.
- **Detection and Diagnosis**: Initial diagnosis indicated a configuration change as the root cause.
- **Resolution**: The team implemented corrective measures, restoring full system functionality within 3.5 hours.
- **Post-Resolution**: System stability was confirmed, and incident closure procedures were completed.

## Root Cause

The root cause of INC-091 was identified as a configuration change within the auth-gateway system. This change was likely introduced during routine updates or maintenance activities associated with Project Fathom, which was completed prior to the incident. The configuration change was not adequately tested or validated before deployment, leading to system disruption.

Alyssa Long responded swiftly as the on-call engineer, and the resolution time of 3.5 hours was within acceptable limits given the circumstances.
