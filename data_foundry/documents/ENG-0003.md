# ENG-0003: Incident Postmortem for INC-067

## Impact

The incident designated INC-067 affected the **auth-worker** system (System ID: SYS-004), which is a critical component within **Project Juniper**. Due to the severity level classified as Sev1, the impact was significant, resulting in service disruption that could potentially impair authentication processes across related services. The incident lasted for approximately 4.5 hours, during which users and internal stakeholders experienced degraded system performance. The disruption posed a risk to operational continuity and highlighted the importance of maintaining system stability within high-criticality environments.

## Timeline

- **2025-01-15**: The incident was reported by Lisa Hensley, Head of Facilities, at approximately [time not specified], indicating an unexpected failure in the auth-worker system.
- **Incident Detection**: The incident was identified promptly following reports of service issues related to authentication.
- **Initial Response**: Crystal Robinson, Project Manager and on-call engineer for auth-worker, was mobilized to investigate the problem.
- **Diagnosis and Resolution**: The root cause was traced to a recent configuration change. Corrective actions were implemented, and the system was restored to normal operation.
- **Resolution Time**: The incident was resolved within 4.5 hours from initial detection.
- **Post-Resolution**: Monitoring confirmed system stability, and incident documentation was completed.

## Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was identified as a configuration change applied to the auth-worker system. This change was categorized under "config_change" and directly led to the service disruption. The change was likely not adequately tested or validated prior to deployment, which resulted in the system's failure to operate as intended. This highlights the importance of rigorous change management procedures, especially for systems with high criticality such as auth-worker.
