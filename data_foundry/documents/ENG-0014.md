# ENG-0014 Incident Postmortem: INC-041

## Impact

The incident designated as INC-041 affected the ledger-worker system (SYS-014), which is integral to Project Fathom. The system's medium criticality status means that its downtime could have impacted financial processing and data integrity within the project scope. The incident resulted in a service disruption lasting approximately 19.5 hours, during which users experienced degraded functionality or unavailability of ledger-related features. Although the incident was contained and resolved, the extended resolution time underscores the importance of addressing hardware reliability to prevent future occurrences.

## Timeline

- **2025-03-26**: The incident was reported by Mark Perez, a Paralegal, indicating issues with the ledger-worker system. Alyssa Long, the on-call Product Manager, was alerted to the problem.
- **Incident Detection**: The hardware failure was identified as the root cause of the system outage.
- **Resolution Duration**: The technical team worked to resolve the hardware failure, which took approximately 19.5 hours.
- **2025-03-26**: The ledger-worker system was fully restored and operational. The incident was documented and closed.

## Root Cause

The root cause of INC-041 was categorized as a hardware failure within the ledger-worker system (SYS-014). This hardware failure led to system downtime and service disruption. The hardware component failure was not specified beyond its classification, but it was significant enough to impair system functionality for nearly a full day.
