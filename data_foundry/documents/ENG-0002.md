# ENG-0002 Incident Postmortem: INC-059

## Impact

The incident identified as INC-059 resulted in a Sev1 classification, indicating a critical impact on the auth-worker system (SYS-004). Given the high criticality of auth-worker within Project Juniper, the disruption affected core authentication functionalities, potentially impacting multiple dependent services and user access. The resolution time was 4.5 hours, during which service availability was compromised, leading to operational delays and potential user dissatisfaction. The incident underscored the importance of maintaining system stability, especially for systems integral to project operations.

## Timeline

- **2025-04-08:** The incident was reported by Jamie Arnold, Head of Data & Analytics, indicating a critical failure in the auth-worker system.
- **Immediately following report:** Crystal Robinson, Project Manager and on-call engineer, was mobilized to address the issue.
- **Incident Response Duration:** The resolution process lasted approximately 4.5 hours.
- **Resolution:** The root cause was identified as a configuration change, which was subsequently reverted or corrected to restore system functionality.
- **Post-resolution:** System stability was confirmed, and normal operations resumed.

## Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was categorized under a configuration change. This change was likely introduced within the auth-worker system, which is built on a Node.js/Express/MongoDB tech stack, and is owned by Project Juniper (P-011). The change was not adequately tested or validated prior to deployment, leading to system disruption. The incident highlights the risks associated with configuration modifications without sufficient safeguards or review processes.

The incident INC-059 on 2025-04-08, caused by a configuration change, underscores the critical importance of strict change management protocols, especially for high-criticality systems such as auth-worker. Crystal Robinson, who responded to the incident, played a key role in resolving the issue within 4.5 hours. Moving forward, the implementation of reinforced procedures and validation mechanisms will be prioritized to prevent recurrence and ensure system resilience for Project Juniper.
