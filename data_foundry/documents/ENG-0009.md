# ENG-0009 Incident Postmortem: INC-049

## Impact

The incident involving the billing-gateway system (SYS-008) on April 16, 2025, resulted in a Sev2 severity classification. Given the mission-critical nature of the billing-gateway, the disruption potentially affected core billing operations and financial processing workflows. The incident lasted for approximately 10.5 hours, during which time service availability was compromised, impacting internal stakeholders and external clients reliant on timely billing data. Although the precise financial or client impact metrics are not available, the extended resolution time underscores the significance of the disruption and the need for process improvements to prevent recurrence.

## Timeline

- **April 16, 2025:** The incident was reported by Stephanie Ross, an accountant within Project Cascade, indicating an operational anomaly in the billing-gateway system.
- **Initial Detection:** The incident was identified and escalated to the on-call engineer, Dylan Miller, a Procurement Specialist based in Singapore, who was responsible for immediate response.
- **Resolution Process:** Over the course of 10.5 hours, Dylan Miller investigated and addressed the root cause, which was classified under deployment_error.
- **Resolution:** The system was restored to normal operation by the end of the resolution period, with all services functioning as intended.

## Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was identified as a deployment_error. This error originated during a recent deployment process within the billing-gateway system, which is built on a Python/FastAPI/PostgreSQL tech stack. The deployment mistake led to system instability, affecting the billing operations critical to Project Cascade, owned by department D-04.
