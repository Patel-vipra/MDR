# ENG-0010: Incident Postmortem for INC-019

## Impact

The incident involving the auth-pipeline system (SYS-005) resulted in a temporary disruption of authentication services, classified as Severity 2 (Sev2). The system's medium criticality meant that while the impact was significant enough to warrant immediate attention, it did not cause widespread outage or critical failure of core business functions. The disruption lasted for approximately 5.5 hours, during which users experienced authentication failures, potentially affecting operational workflows within Project Zephyr. The incident was reported by Brian Ramirez, Procurement Specialist, and addressed promptly to restore normal service.

## Timeline

- **2025-04-07**: The incident was reported by Brian Ramirez, Procurement Specialist, indicating authentication issues with the auth-pipeline system.
- **Immediately following report**: On-call engineer Daniel Kennedy was engaged to investigate the issue.
- **Resolution**: The root cause was identified as a configuration change applied to the auth-pipeline system, which was subsequently reverted or corrected.
- **Total resolution time**: 5.5 hours from initial report to full service restoration.

Throughout this period, communication was maintained between the on-call engineer Daniel Kennedy and relevant stakeholders to ensure timely updates and resolution.

## Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was categorized under a "config_change." A recent configuration change within the auth-pipeline system (SYS-005) led to the authentication failure. The change was likely implemented without sufficient validation or review, resulting in unintended disruption of the system's functionality. This configuration change was the direct trigger for the Sev2 incident, highlighting the importance of rigorous change management procedures.
