# ENG-0012 - Incident Postmortem for INC-027

## Impact

The incident involving the billing-worker system (SYS-009) was classified as Severity Level 2 (Sev2). Although the system's criticality is designated as low, the disruption lasted for approximately 12.5 hours, during which the system experienced deployment-related issues. The incident was reported by Debra Davidson, a Sales Executive, on February 13, 2025. The disruption potentially affected billing operations within the scope of Project Lattice, which is currently active under project owner E-033 and department D-03. Despite the low criticality, the extended resolution time underscored the importance of refining deployment procedures to prevent similar occurrences in the future.

## Timeline

- **February 13, 2025:** The incident was reported by Debra Davidson regarding the billing-worker system.
- **Initial Detection:** The issue was identified during routine monitoring, prompting immediate investigation.
- **Response Activation:** Angelica Keith, serving as the on-call engineer for SYS-009, was notified and began troubleshooting.
- **Resolution:** The deployment error was rectified, and the system was restored to operational status after approximately 12.5 hours.
- **Post-Resolution:** A review was initiated to determine root cause and prevent recurrence.

## Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was determined to be a deployment error. This error originated during a recent deployment process for the billing-worker system, which is part of Project Lattice. The deployment mistake was not caught during pre-deployment checks, leading to the system malfunction. The deployment error falls under the category of deployment_error, highlighting procedural gaps in deployment validation and oversight.
