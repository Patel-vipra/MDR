ENG-0001 Incident Postmortem: INC-017
Impact
The incident identified as INC-017 affected the auth-api system, which is part of Project Titan. The auth-api system, owned by project P-021, has a criticality classified as medium. The incident was classified as Sev1, indicating a severe impact on system functionality. The primary consequence was a disruption in authentication services, which could have affected user access and internal operations reliant on the auth-api. The resolution of this incident was achieved within 1.5 hours, minimizing prolonged service disruption.
Timeline
- 2025-02-09: The incident was reported by Daniel Wagner, Head of Legal, via the internal monitoring and incident reporting channels.
- Incident Detection: The issue was identified as a result of a configuration change that led to the failure.
- Response Activation: Cameron Parker, a Financial Analyst and the on-call engineer for auth-api, was alerted to the incident.
- Resolution: The root cause was addressed through a configuration correction, restoring normal operation.
- Resolution Time: The incident was resolved within 1.5 hours from detection.
Root Cause
The root cause of INC-017 was a configuration change within the auth-api system. This change was identified as the primary factor leading to the service disruption. The configuration modification was not properly validated before deployment, resulting in the system's failure to operate correctly. This category of root cause falls under "config_change," highlighting the importance of rigorous change management processes.
