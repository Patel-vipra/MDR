ENG-0008 Incident Postmortem: INC-110
Impact
The incident involving the auth-api system, identified as INC-110, resulted in a Sev1 severity level disruption. The auth-api, a medium-criticality system utilizing Python/Django/MySQL, experienced an outage that affected core authentication services. The disruption lasted for approximately 1.5 hours, during which users were unable to access authentication functionalities. Cristian Santos, Head of Engineering, reported the incident, which temporarily impacted Project Titan's operational capacity. The swift resolution minimized overall business impact, but highlighted vulnerabilities in change management procedures.
Timeline
- 2025-02-06: The incident was reported by Cristian Santos, Head of Engineering, indicating a critical failure in the auth-api system.
- Immediate Response: Cameron Parker, on-call engineer and Financial Analyst, was alerted and initiated incident response procedures.
- Resolution: The root cause was identified as a configuration change applied to auth-api. The change was reverted, restoring system functionality.
- Resolution Time: The incident was resolved within 1.5 hours from initial report.
Root Cause
The root cause of INC-110 was classified as a configuration change. An unauthorized or improperly documented change was implemented to auth-api, which led to the system's failure. This change was not adequately tested or reviewed prior to deployment, resulting in the outage. The incident underscores the importance of strict change management protocols, especially for systems critical to Project Titan.
