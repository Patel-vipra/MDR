ENG-0004 Incident Postmortem: INC-080
Impact
The incident designated as INC-080 affected the auth-gateway system (SYS-003), which plays a critical role within Project Fathom. The auth-gateway's medium criticality status indicates that its disruption posed a significant risk to authentication services across multiple platforms. The incident resulted in a service outage lasting 4.5 hours, impacting end-users' ability to authenticate and access core services. This downtime potentially led to customer support escalations and operational delays, underscoring the importance of maintaining system stability and integrity.
Timeline
- 2025-05-04: The incident was reported by Monica Herrera, Head of Customer Support, at approximately the time of occurrence.
- Incident Detection: The issue was identified internally and escalated promptly.
- Response Initiation: Alyssa Long, the on-call Product Manager for auth-gateway, was mobilized to address the incident.
- Resolution: The root cause was identified as a configuration change applied to the auth-gateway system, which led to the outage.
- Resolution Time: The incident was resolved within 4.5 hours from detection.
- Post-Resolution: Monitoring confirmed system stability, and services were restored to normal operation.
Root Cause
The root cause of INC-080 was categorized as a configuration change. This change was implemented within the auth-gateway system (SYS-003), which is part of Project Fathom. The change was not sufficiently validated prior to deployment, leading to a misconfiguration that disrupted authentication services. The incident underscores the importance of rigorous change management procedures, especially for systems with medium criticality like auth-gateway.
