Executive Summary
This report provides an overview of the budget allocation and expenditure for Project Monsoon during the fiscal quarter FY25-Q3. The total allocated amount for the project was 1,486,447.00, with a current expenditure of 445,934.1. The project is currently under review, with expenditures subject to further validation.
Budget Overview
The budget for Project Monsoon (Budget ID: B-040) was approved with an allocated amount of 1,486,447.00. The project commenced on February 6, 2025, and has since been progressing towards its objectives within the allocated financial scope.
